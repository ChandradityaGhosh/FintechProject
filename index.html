The NMHS promise

From first steps to future leaders.

National Model High School is under G.B. Memorial Group and affiliated with W.B.B.S.E. and W.B.C.H.S.E. The school was established on 2 April 1994 and presents itself as an English-medium school focused on holistic education, academic excellence and co-curricular growth.

01 • Early Years

Lower Nursery to Primary

Curiosity, language, discipline, confidence and happy classroom habits.

02 • Middle School

Class V to VIII

Structured learning, communication, responsibility and steady academic growth.

03 • Secondary

Class IX to X

Board preparation through focused curriculum, teacher guidance and consistent evaluation.

04 • Higher Secondary

Class XI to XII

Science, Commerce and Arts streams for focused future pathways.

Academic excellence gallery

Strong academics, modern learning spaces.

NMHS highlights English-medium instruction, W.B.B.S.E. / W.B.C.H.S.E. curriculum, smart classroom infrastructure, library and laboratories.

Curriculum

LN to Class X

1st Language: Bengali / Hindi. 2nd Language: English. 3rd Language: Hindi / Punjabi / Bengali. Other subjects include History, Geography, Mathematics, Physical Science, Life Science, Work Education and Physical Education.

Science Stream

Science

Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Computer Science and Biological Science for Class XI–XII students.

Commerce & Arts

Commerce / Arts

Commerce includes Accountancy, Business Organization, Economics, Computer Application and related options. Arts includes Economics, Political Science, History, Computer Application, Geography, Education and Philosophy.
